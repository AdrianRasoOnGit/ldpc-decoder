We have no need of other worlds. We need mirrors. We don't know what to do with other worlds. A single world, our own, suffices us; but we can't accept it for what it is. We are searchers after man, and man alone. We have no need of other worlds, we need mirrors. We seek contact, but we will never find it. We are in the foolish position of a man straining for a goal he fears, and which he has no need of. Man is seeking to conquer the cosmos, but he wants only to extend the boundaries of Earth to the frontiers of the cosmos.
